# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/`: how the brand sounds (voice guide and brand rules).
- `leads/`: the list of practice customers and their groups.
- `campaigns/`: one folder per campaign, with its plan and emails.
- `notes/`: research, hand-off notes and scratch work.

## Rules

- Write in plain English.
- Never send email to real people.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
