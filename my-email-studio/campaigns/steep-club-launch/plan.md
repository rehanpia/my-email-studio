# The Steep Club: Launch Plan

Draft written without the marketing plugin. Compare it with the output of /marketing:campaign-plan.

## 1. Goal
300 waitlist members start a subscription by **Sunday 3 January 2027** (the end of the first month of the launch). That is 15% of about 2,000 people, which is ambitious.

## 2. Audience
About 2,000 people who joined the Kettlebird Tea waitlist, in three groups. Group sizes are not known yet, so confirm them before sending.

| Group | Who they are | What they probably want |
|---|---|---|
| Tea-curious beginners | New to loose-leaf tea | Easy, friendly start, no jargon |
| Gift buyers | Buying for someone else | Something thoughtful, simple to give |
| Daily tea drinkers | Already drink tea every day | New teas and variety |

## 3. Key messages
- A new tea arrives every month, so you never run out of things to try.
- It is easy: sign up once, and the box comes to you.
- Each group hears the same offer with a different opening line and example.
- Plain English only. No price, discount or health claim appears in this plan. Add any price or offer after you have confirmed it.

## 4. Channels
Email only. No paid ads.

## 5. Timeline and email calendar
Four weeks, starting Monday 7 December 2026.

| # | Send date | Audience | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mon 7 Dec 2026 | All three groups | Announce The Steep Club and invite people to join |
| 2 | Mon 14 Dec 2026 | All three groups | Explain how the box works and what is inside |
| 3 | Mon 21 Dec 2026 | Gift buyers lead, all groups receive | Show it as a gift and say what to do about delivery dates |
| 4 | Mon 28 Dec 2026 | All three groups | Closing reminder, only if the sign-up deadline is real |

## 6. Personalization
Each email gets one version per group, with the same structure, subject, link and footer. Only the opening line and one example change.

## 7. Metrics
- Subscriptions started (the goal: 300).
- Click-through rate on the main button, per email and per group.
- Unsubscribes per email.

## 8. Risks
- 15% is a high target. Decide in week 2 whether to adjust the goal or the offer.
- Late December is busy for gifts and delivery. Check delivery dates before email 3.
- Fake urgency would hurt trust. Only mention a deadline that is real.

## 9. Open questions for you
- How many people are in each group?
- What is the real sign-up deadline, if any?
- What does the box cost and contain? (Leave out until confirmed.)
